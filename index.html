About MyKabira

Thirteen years, twenty-five thousand travellers, and one belief: a good trip is designed, not sold.

Our Story

It started with one badly planned holiday

In 2013 our founder came back from a trip to Ladakh that had looked perfect on paper and fallen apart in practice — wrong season, wrong altitude schedule, a hotel two hours from everything worth seeing. The problem was not the destination. It was that nobody planning the trip had ever been there.

MyKabira was built to fix exactly that. We started as a two-person desk in Andheri, planning trips for friends of friends. The rule then is the rule now: nobody sells an itinerary they have not walked themselves, or checked with someone on the ground who has.

Today we are a team of 24 consultants, ground coordinators and visa specialists across three cities, with partner networks in 40 countries. We still plan trips one conversation at a time.

🎯

Our Mission

To make well-planned travel accessible — trips that respect your time, your budget and the places you visit.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the travel partner Indian families call first — for the honeymoon, the anniversary, and the trip after that.

🤝

Our Promise

Transparent pricing, no hidden margins, and a human on the phone within an hour — wherever in the world you are.
